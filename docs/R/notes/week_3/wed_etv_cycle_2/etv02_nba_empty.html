0 · Context & ethics (8 min)

Our data are Kobe Bryant’s game-level records compiled from Basketball-Reference. Before extracting anything:

  • Source & permission: we did not collect these box scores. Check the site’s terms and robots.txt; today’s workbook was prepared for you, and its variable_names sheet documents the fields.
  • Misuse without context (CLO 12.2): raw point totals can mislead when seasons have different lengths or a player misses games.

One sentence: name a way sports stats get misread when context is dropped.

1 · The central question

How did Kobe Bryant’s scoring performance change across his first five NBA seasons?

We’ll measure performance as points per minute. A rate lets us compare seasons with different numbers of games and minutes.
